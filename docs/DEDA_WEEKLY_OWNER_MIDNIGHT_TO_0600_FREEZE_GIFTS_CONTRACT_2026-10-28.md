## Owner-approved intended behavior
- Competition closes at **00:00:00 Asia/Baghdad** at the agreed weekly boundary. The league score for the closing week accepts only **trusted server-confirmed eligible XP before the cutoff**; authoritative server time is used, never phone time.
- **00:00–05:59:59 Iraq time** is a six-hour *league-only review/frozen results window*. The final top ten and podium (ranks 1–3) preserve the exact order/points reached at closing time; participants can inspect and screenshot the final chart. Show clear badge/copy `النتائج النهائية — توزيع الجوائز الساعة 6 صباحاً` and a countdown. No rank motion, no new league points, no re-ranks from later actions or app relaunch, no midnight deletion of screenshotable results.
- Normal daily tasks, earned original LEVEL XP, original task points, existing account balances and normal in-app activity must **continue normally** during this interval. Task actions between 00:00 and 06:00 do **not earn competitive weekly-league XP for either week** under the requested pause; never suppress, reverse, or reset their real level/task XP. Any future alternative carry-forward policy requires explicit owner review and different tests.
- At **06:00:00 Asia/Baghdad**, unlock/open the next weekly competition with a **new league-only** weekly score (zero), preserving all account history, earned XP/levels, owned inventory, old wallet amounts, original identifiers and premium equipment. The closed week remains selectable as **الأسبوع السابق**, and its ranked top ten feed the already specified monthly admin history archive. Never overwrite an old immutable result.
- At the new week's 06:00 opening, send **exactly one league reward gift per qualifying winner** of the immutable previous result: #1=150 diamonds; #2=100; #3=50; #4–#10=10 each; other places=0. Maximum 370 diamonds for a full verified ten-person result; fewer winners means only eligible winners are rewarded.
- The earned prize must show under the winner's existing **هداياي** inbox, labeled `هدية دوري المتصدرين — الأسبوع المنتهي`, with rank, amount, week/date, and state `بانتظار الاستلام`. In line with existing inbox mechanics, **the user explicitly taps استلام الهدية**, then the verified authorized diamond wallet is credited only once. Inbox display alone must not silently mint, spend or auto-claim gems. Sender/source is trusted DEDA League, not a fake human sender or owner wallet transfer.
- League prizes do **not** debit the general manager's personal 1M diamonds, admin gift allowance, any competitor account or previous balance; do not build a new parallel spendable balance. Reuse the existing audited authorized receipt/claim path only after it is proved suitable for this new system-generated prize type; otherwise leave payouts disabled until independently reviewed backend design.
- Receipt key binds canonical winner account and immutable week, e.g. `(weekId, canonicalAccountUid, league_prize)`; use durable server-side immutable final snapshot + transactional exactly-once issuance and exactly-once gift redemption, robust to 2 devices, repeated scheduler runs, offline/reconnect, retries, and 06:00 scheduler delays.
- Client input, public DEDA alias, phone clock or manager button cannot pick standings, alter a closed snapshot, assign rewards or trigger prize issuance. Restrict snapshots, ownership, reward receipt reads/writes and monthly admin history to appropriate authenticated server permissions.
- If settlement is delayed or unsafe, show `توزيع الجوائز قيد التحقق`, keep the frozen previous-week result, do NOT duplicate or fabricate gifts, and allow the new 06:00 league to begin independently after the calendar boundary. Backfill genuine pending gifts idempotently only after validation.
- Time-boundary test coverage: 23:59:59 → 00:00:00 → 05:59:59 → 06:00:00, clocks in Baghdad, ranking ties, last-millisecond points, delayed and duplicated task events, 2 devices one account, winner changes forbidden after freeze, gift pending/redeemed once, app restart, offline start, 1–10 winners, no real balances changed by test fixtures.

## Owner decision — flexible launch day / seven-calendar-day recurrence
- Confirmed by the owner on 2026-10-28: **no particular weekday is required** (not Monday, Friday or any preset weekday). The **official server-recorded launch date** defines the inaugural competition weekday. The same weekly midnight closing boundary repeats every **seven calendar days** in `Asia/Baghdad`, derived only from that immutable server anchor, not from phone clock or activity of the first user who happens to open the app. The owner does not need to choose a weekday in advance.
- On each recurring boundary: **00:00 final close/freeze** → **00:00–05:59:59 display unchanged final top ten** → **06:00 new league round starts + eligible pending prizes issued exactly once**. This is a seven-day **calendar recurrence** that intentionally includes a six-hour scoring pause, **not a promise of 168 continuously eligible scoring hours per round**; ordinary XP/tasks continue during the pause. The product UI should make the actual active window clear to users.
- Determine the first official start and its closing midnight atomically on the authoritative server when the league is activated (one canonical start marker). If official activation happens mid-day, do not silently shorten/extend the first competition or award prizes for an incomplete period; stage a clear first-round start at the next server-designated 06:00 and publish the effective dates before opening. Do not change the anchor after legitimate competitions have started.
- Stage17's Monday midnight was private QA-only and must not determine the live competition weekday. Test week-to-week date rollover, month/year rollover, DST-safe local-calendar arithmetic, next-day post-midnight scoring pause, and official backend clock, including first-round activation and two-device coherence.
